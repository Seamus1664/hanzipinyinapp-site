Draft testing

Leave the Hanzi to Pinyin Draft Test

You can stop participating in the Marketplace draft test at any time.

Request removal from the tester list

Email hanzipinyin@outlook.com from the Google account you used for testing. Use the subject Draft Tester Opt-Out and ask us to remove that email address from the Hanzi to Pinyin draft tester list.

What happens next: We will remove the requested Google account from the draft tester list. We use the email address only to identify and remove your tester access.

Uninstall the test add-on

If Hanzi to Pinyin is installed, you can also remove it through your Google Workspace Marketplace account:

  1. Open Google Workspace Marketplace.
  2. Open Manage apps.
  3. Find Hanzi to Pinyin and choose Uninstall.
