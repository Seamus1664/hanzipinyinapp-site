Hanzi to Pinyin

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026. Applies to version 1.1 and later using the bundled conversion engine.

Hanzi to Pinyin converts text within Google Apps Script using a bundled Pinyin dictionary. It does not send source text or results to an external translation or romanization service, and it does not retain conversion text or results in a persistent or cross-execution cache.

1. Information processed

The add-on processes values supplied to the PINYIN formula and cells selected for menu conversion. Menu conversion also checks the adjacent destination area, including whether it contains values or formulas, is merged, or can be edited, to protect existing data. It processes your choices for tone style and capitalization. Version 1.1.1 and later always require confirmation before overwriting existing values or formulas. Converted Pinyin is derived output. The add-on does not create aggregate or anonymized datasets from spreadsheet content.

2. Where processing happens

Conversion runs on Google's Apps Script platform, not entirely on your device. Google Sheets supplies the input and receives the output. The add-on contains its own conversion library and dictionary; no separate conversion API, external network request, or developer-operated server is used for conversion.

3. Storage and retention

Source text is handled during execution, and converted output remains in your spreadsheet or formula result until you change or remove it. The add-on does not create a separate database or persistent or cross-execution conversion cache. During a single execution, the converter may temporarily reuse results for repeated text; those results are not retained for later executions. Your tone and capitalization preferences, together with a compatibility setting that enforces overwrite confirmation, are saved separately for your account in Google Apps Script User Properties. Restore defaults deletes your saved Hanzi to Pinyin preferences from Apps Script User Properties and returns the add-on to its default settings. It does not delete your Google account, spreadsheet, or generated output.

Google may retain platform execution and error records under its own service policies. The add-on does not deliberately log source text or conversion results. Information you choose to email to support is received by the developer's support mailbox; please do not send confidential spreadsheet content. Support correspondence is retained while your request is active and deleted from our support mailbox within 90 days after resolution, unless longer retention is legally required. You may request earlier deletion at hanzipinyin@outlook.com. Microsoft may retain recoverable copies under its applicable retention policies.

4. Permissions

  • spreadsheets.currentonly: read selected/input cells and write converted output in the spreadsheet where the add-on is used.
  • script.container.ui: display Settings, Usage Guide, and dialogs inside Google Sheets.
  • Email and basic profile permissions are included by Google Workspace Marketplace for installation and account identity. The conversion code does not read your email address or profile.

The add-on does not request external-request permission. Google presents the permissions during authorization. No separate Hanzi to Pinyin account is required.

5. Use and sharing

Google user data is used to provide the conversion and settings features described here. We do not sell or rent it, use it for advertising, or use spreadsheet content to train AI models. The developer does not receive spreadsheet content through normal conversion. The add-on does not transfer source text or converted output to external conversion or AI services. Google processes data as the provider of Sheets and Apps Script under its applicable terms and policies. We may handle support information you voluntarily provide or disclose information if legally required.

6. Google API user data

Hanzi to Pinyin's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

7. Your choices and security

You control when to run conversion. You can restore default preferences in Settings, delete formulas or output from Sheets, uninstall the add-on, and revoke its access in your Google Account. Uninstalling does not delete generated spreadsheet content. Google controls platform retention. The add-on limits spreadsheet access to the spreadsheet where it is used, stores preferences separately for each user in Apps Script User Properties, and operates without a developer-run conversion server or conversion database. It relies on Google's access controls and infrastructure; no electronic processing can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Website and support

This information website does not request access to your spreadsheets and contains no developer-added analytics or advertising scripts. The hosting provider may process ordinary web-request information. Support correspondence is handled through Outlook email.

9. Children and updates

This educational productivity tool is not directed specifically to children under 13. Schools, teachers, parents, and guardians are responsible for appropriate use. We update this page and its effective date when data practices change.

10. Earlier versions

Version 1.0 used a Google-hosted conversion endpoint and could temporarily cache results for up to six hours. Those practices do not describe version 1.1. The current Marketplace draft uses version 1.1.1; public availability remains subject to Google's review.
